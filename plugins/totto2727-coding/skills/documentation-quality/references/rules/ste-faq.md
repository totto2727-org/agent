# STE FAQ migration note

The [official FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html) remains useful explanatory guidance.
The current catalog uses the complete supplied Issue 9 source rather than four FAQ-only definitions.
Use the full-source checks and allowances below, not duplicate FAQ checks.

| Former ID                         | Current check | Source-backed interpretation                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ste-faq-procedural-instructions` | `ste-5-3`     | Direct commands for reader actions, with the source's important-condition and safety emphasis allowance             |
| `ste-faq-active-description`      | `ste-3-6`     | Active voice in both writing modes, unknown-agent passive only in descriptions, with state adjectives distinguished |
| `ste-faq-condition-first`         | `ste-5-4`     | Necessary pre-action condition before its command, separated by an accurately placed comma                          |
| `ste-faq-one-topic`               | `ste-4-1`     | Concrete clear sentences and one descriptive idea per sentence, developed under `ste-6-1`                           |

These mappings replace the former IDs in saved manifests.
They do not redefine all sentences as procedures or make every conditional clause come first.
Do not claim a pass for an unavailable dictionary entry, actor, action meaning, or claimed exception.
The [Issue 9 operational map](ste-issue9.md) contains the verified printed pages, all numbered rules, and original rubrics.
The [coverage record](../coverage.md) records bounded automated coverage and separate-review obligations.
